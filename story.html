My Story

My story began long before I ever walked the halls of Archer High. I am the product of sacrifices I didn’t always see but always felt. My family carried dreams across oceans and state lines, and every step I take rests on the strength they passed down to me.

High school wasn’t just classes and grades — it was discovering who I am, where I come from, and what I’m capable of. As I step into my next chapter, I carry my culture, my family’s pride, and my own ambition with me. This is only the beginning, and I’m ready for what comes next.

— Jay
